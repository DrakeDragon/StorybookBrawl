# Finished roster release

The active roster contains 169 heroes and 102 enemies, all with dedicated combat artwork. All 25 realms have painted maps and wide and portrait arenas. The full 750-stage campaign and its harder repeat remain available.

New recruitment draws only from the 169 active heroes. Older saves retain all previously owned heroes, upgrades, squads and presets. Previously owned heroes outside the active roster remain selectable and appear in the collection. They do not increase active collection totals. Collection milestones now fit the smaller roster, and existing claim identifiers are preserved.

Encounter pools use the 102 painted enemies. All 25 original realm bosses remain in their original realms. Smaller enemy pools repeat more often.

Verified in a simulated browser environment: scripts initialize, every rarity has recruitable heroes, a save containing all 250 original heroes retains all 250 and their upgrades, legacy squads and presets survive migration, collection goals are reachable, and stages 1 through 1500 generate valid painted enemies with the original realm bosses. Fresh collection rendering also passes.

Every original artwork file is preserved. Every image decodes, every direct asset reference resolves, and the full release ZIP passes its integrity check.
