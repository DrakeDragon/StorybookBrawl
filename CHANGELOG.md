# Changelog

## Garden and Combat Update

- Added a painted Garden of Beatrix map, wide and portrait garden arenas, and dedicated combat sprites for all 10 heroes and six enemies in the realm.
- Added Oberon's illustrated Draw card and combat sprites, including dedicated Dream Quake and Midsummer Spell sequences.
- Reworked the battle command panel with four larger move buttons for the active hero, named cooldowns and unlock levels, and an ultimate charge indicator.
- Added a compact two-column matchup layout for larger squads and enemy lineups.
- Fixed import of older save codes so missing progression fields are restored when the code loads.
- Dedicated combat sprites now cover 81 heroes and 49 enemies across the game.

## Major Graphics Update

- Added an illustrated title screen, a campaign atlas, a redesigned Adventure briefing, and larger side-by-side lineups.
- Redesigned card draws and added a searchable, filterable catalog of all 250 possible pulls.
- Illustrated the first seven realm maps and battle arenas, from Sherwood Forest through Wild Wood and River Bank. Looking-Glass Land, Neverland, Hundred Acre Wood and Wild Wood and River Bank also have portrait arena compositions for narrow screens.
- Added dedicated basic and charged combat sprite sheets for 70 heroes and 43 enemies, including the full rosters of the first seven realms. Most special moves share their character's charged sequence.
- Added arrows, spell trails, hit and healing effects, shield effects, and named enemy charge cues. Some animation respects the device's reduced-motion setting.
- Packaged the game and its WebP assets for offline play or static hosting. See the README for launch and save-transfer instructions.

## v4.1 - The Unwritten
- Removed the hidden unlock code.
- Added a full story: 750 chapters (one per stage) across 25 realms, 25 restored Pages, a Narrator mystery and a finale.
- Boss and realm-opening story scenes; Menu → Story replay log.
- Added profiles (epithet, quote, backstory) for all 250 heroes.
- Rebuilt the hero screen into Profile, Stats, Moves and Gear tabs with a stats card.
- Reworked navigation to 5 tabs (Adventure, Heroes, Draw, Gear, Menu).
- Added a Today dashboard, a feature tile grid with badges, unlock hints and a Help & Guide.
- Locked heroes now show their home realm.

## v4.0 - American and British tales
- Reworked to 250 heroes (including side characters) across 25 realms.
- Extreme rarity, long collection time, tickets every 3rd stage, Fate's Favor bonus cards.
- New moves for every hero, themed gear and sets, 47 bonds.
- New save key `storybookBrawl_v4`.
